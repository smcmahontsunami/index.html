Calculation logic (for Sigma / Snowflake build)

target_gal = cars × target_gpc

gal_variance = gallons − target_gal  (+ = over target)

cars_supported = gallons ÷ target_gpc

car_variance = cars − cars_supported  (− = short; equals −gal_variance ÷ target_gpc)

excess_cost = max(gal_variance, 0) ÷ 1000 × $/kgal

Closed hours (0 cars) count fully as variance — that water has no car behind it (leaks, RO reject, reclaim top-off, stuck valves). Target GPC should be a site-level parameter so equipment packages can carry different targets.
